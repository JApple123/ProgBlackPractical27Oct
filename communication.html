Communication

By far, one of computing's most prominent contributions to society is improvement to the speed and security of communication all over the world. Prior to the creation of the internet, communication over long distances was predominantly done through mail delivery (physically and usually domestically), and remotely over great distances through telegraphs

Advantages of Modern Communication

The creation of the internet allowed for fast and secure transmission of data over great distances. In the early internet, this took the form of email but has since developed to much more, including social media, texting and blogs.

When talking about the effect of computing on global communication, it is important to compare modern communication to earlier forms.

Convenience

Before the creation of the internet, the most common form of communication was sending letters by mail. This involved physically writing the letter, packaging it in an envelope, stamping the envelope and sending it off to be physically transported to the recipient. Despite the highly-organized efficiency of postal services in many countries, this was still a cumbersome process

In comparison, modern forms of communication such as SMS texting, phone calls and social media are much more simple. For texting, all it takes is to find a contact, write a message and press send. This makes modern communication significantly more convenient for the user.

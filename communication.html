Positive impacts of computer science

Communication

Computing has greatly increased the speed and reach of communication around the world. Before the internet, people relied on physical mail for many long-distance messages and telegraphs for rapid communication across great distances.

Advantages of modern communication

The internet made it possible to send information quickly across long distances. Early internet communication included email; today it also includes social media, messaging, voice and video calls, and blogs.

These tools are easier to use and much faster than many earlier ways of communicating.

Convenience

Sending a letter traditionally meant writing it, putting it in an envelope, adding a stamp and waiting for it to be delivered. Postal services made this process reliable, but it still took time and several steps.

With modern communication, people can find a contact, write a message and send it in moments. Texting, phone calls and social media make it simpler to keep in touch.

Speed

Digital messages can reach someone on the other side of the world in seconds. This helps people share urgent information, coordinate work and stay connected across time zones.

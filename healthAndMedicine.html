Positive impacts of computer science

Health and medicine

Computer science has allowed for multiple advances in the medical field, such as the Da Vinci Robot for surgery, the use of machine learning in identifying different health issues, and expanding the opportunity for remote health care through linked health monitors like insulin pumps and fitness trackers.

Da Vinci Robot


Firstly the Da Vinci robot:
This is a precision surgery tool that allows surgeons to use small controllers that move the arms of the machine at a smaller scale, and with filtering for hand tremors. The robot also has a high-definition view of the surgical site to allow the surgeon to operate effectively.

Computer-assisted surgery

The da Vinci surgical system lets surgeons control small instruments through hand controllers. The system can scale movements and filter hand tremors, while a high-definition view helps the surgeon see the operating area in detail.

Other uses in healthcare

Machine learning can help clinicians examine medical images and identify patterns that may need attention. Connected health devices can also record measurements and share them with care teams, supporting remote monitoring between appointments.

Machine Learning


Machine Learning also has a use in Medicine, as it allows for the detection of some illness before it becomes a major problem. For example, the company Skin Analytics uses dermatoscopic images taken with smartphone attachments to triage and rule out ksin cancer. This device is called DERM, and is deployed across numerous NHS trusts in the UK. Other similar products include DermaSensor, an FDA-cleared medical device that uses Elastic Scattering Spectroscopy and machine learning to help evaluate suspicious skin lesions for melanoma and other cancers.

Positive impacts of computer science

Health and medicine

Computer science has allowed for multiple advances in the medical field, such as the Da Vinci Robot for surgery, the use of machine learning in identifying different health issues, and expanding the opportunity for remote health care through linked health monitors like insulin pumps and fitness trackers.

Computer-assisted surgery

The Da Vinci robot is a precision surgery tool that allows surgeons to use small controllers that move the arms of the machine at a smaller scale and filter hand tremors. The robot also has a high-definition view of the surgical site to help the surgeon operate effectively.

Machine learning

Machine learning can help detect some illnesses before they become a major problem. For example, Skin Analytics uses dermatoscopic images taken with smartphone attachments to triage and rule out skin cancer. Its DERM device is deployed across numerous NHS trusts in the UK. Other products include DermaSensor, an FDA-cleared device that uses elastic scattering spectroscopy and machine learning to help evaluate suspicious skin lesions for melanoma and other cancers.

Other uses

Electronic health records

Store and organise patient medical histories, test results and treatment plans in a digital format for easier access across providers.

Medical imaging

Power diagnostic equipment such as CT scans, MRIs and ultrasounds to create detailed views of internal anatomy.

Patient monitoring

Track vital signs in intensive care units and trigger automated alerts for medical staff if complications arise.
